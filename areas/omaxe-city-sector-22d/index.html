Area Guide

Omaxe City, Sector 22D, Rohtak

An integrated township on Jhajjar Road from a national developer — and one of the more established addresses in Rohtak for buyers who want everything working on day one.

The address

Omaxe City sits in Sector 22D on the Jhajjar Road side of Rohtak. It is an integrated township, which means the internal roads, security, common-area power and daily-needs retail are inside the boundary and managed rather than left to the municipality.

For buyers it is the straightforward option: an established development from a national builder, with families already living there. That last part matters more than most buyers realise — an occupied township functions, and you can see it functioning before you buy.

What we see here

Residential plots, independent floors and built houses all trade in this township. Which one suits you depends on whether you are building, moving in, or holding. Our plots and independent floors pages set out the difference properly.

What to check in any township purchase

  • Current maintenance charge, in writing, and how it has moved.
  • Who manages the township now — the developer, or an owners’ association.
  • Power backup for common areas and whether it extends into homes.
  • Water supply and how it has held up through summer.
  • The specific unit’s title and dues, which are separate from the township’s overall standing.
We do not publish prices or availability here, because both change weekly and a stale number helps nobody. Call or message and you will get the current position for the size you are after.

How it compares

Against Suncity, Omaxe City is generally the more established address with the longer occupancy history. Against the HSVP sectors, you pay for the managed environment and gain predictability. We will give you a straight comparison for your budget rather than pushing whichever we happen to have inventory in.

Common questions

Is Omaxe City a good place to buy in Rohtak?

It suits buyers who want a managed, gated environment with amenities working from day one, and who are comfortable carrying a monthly maintenance charge. Buyers who prefer lower running costs and are happy managing their own property often do better in an established HSVP sector. We will tell you which group you are in.

Can I buy a plot in Omaxe City and build myself?

Plots do trade in the township, and building is subject to the sanctioned plan and the township’s own building rules. Those rules are worth reading before you buy, because they govern what you can build and how.
