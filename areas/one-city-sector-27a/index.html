The address

One City is a planned development in Sector 27A. It appeals to buyers who want a compact, self-contained layout where daily needs are close by, rather than a plot on an open stretch waiting for the area to fill in.

Buying in a newer development

Newer addresses can be good value at entry, because you are buying before the area has fully established. The risk is the mirror of that: you are relying on development continuing. So the checks matter more here than in a settled sector.

  • What exists today, walked and seen. Not the master plan. Go and look at the roads, services and built units that are actually there.
  • The licence and layout approval, and that your specific plot falls inside the approved area.
  • The developer’s delivery record on their earlier projects.
  • How many units are sold and how many are occupied. Occupancy is a better signal than sales.
  • What is committed in writing versus what is being described verbally.
We will not repeat a developer’s timeline as fact. We will tell you what the site looks like now and what the same developer’s previous projects actually took to deliver.

Who it suits

Buyers who want a newer address and are prepared to be early, and investors comfortable with a multi-year hold. If you need an established neighbourhood with schools and shops already running, the HSVP sectors or Omaxe City will serve you better, and we will say so.

Common questions

Is it risky to buy in a newer development?

It carries a different risk profile, not necessarily a higher one, provided the approvals are in order and the developer has delivered before. The real risk is buying on a drawing. Verify the licence, verify the plot falls inside the approved layout, and walk the site. Then it is a reasonable decision made with open eyes.

What happens if the development does not progress?

You own the land either way, assuming title is clean - which is exactly why we check title before anything else. But liquidity suffers and resale gets harder if the area stalls. That is the honest downside, and it is why we ask how long you intend to hold.

Ask about availability

Tell us the size and budget you have in mind. If we have something that fits we will send details the same day — and if we do not, we will say so.
