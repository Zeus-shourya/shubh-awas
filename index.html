About Us

The Man Whose Name Is On It

Shubh Awas Properties was built on a simple, slightly old-fashioned idea: that the person who sells you land should still take your call three years later.

Jagvender Siwach founded Shubh Awas Properties after years spent watching families in Haryana get the same raw deal — a glossy brochure, a rushed site visit, an agent who disappeared the day the token money cleared. He set out to run the opposite kind of firm. One where the title is checked before the plot is shown. Where the price you are quoted is the price on the paperwork. Where nobody is talked into a purchase they will regret at registry.

He knows this market the way you only can by working it in person — the sectors that are actually developing, the layouts where possession is real, the builders who deliver and the ones who only promise. That knowledge is the whole product. Everything else is paperwork and follow-through.

“A house is the biggest thing most families will ever buy. They deserve to be told the truth about it, even when the truth costs me the sale.”

Jagvender Siwach Founder & Managing Director
  • He shows you the plot, not the render. Every site visit is on the ground, on the actual khasra, with the layout plan in hand.
  • Title first, sale second. Ownership chain, mutation, approvals and encumbrance are verified before a client is asked for anything.
  • One point of contact. From first enquiry to registry and possession, you deal with the same desk — not a rotating call centre.
  • Plain numbers. Rate, EDC, IDC, PLC, stamp duty and registry costs written out in full before you commit to anything.

How We Work

Four Things We Will Not Compromise

01

Title Before Talk

Ownership chain, mutation records, approvals and encumbrance are checked before a property reaches our list. If it does not clear, we do not sell it.

02

The Full Number

Rate, EDC, IDC, PLC, stamp duty, registry and any maintenance corpus — written out before you commit, so nothing lands on you at the sub-registrar.

03

The Site, As It Is

Site visits happen on the ground, at the actual location, with the layout plan open. What you can see is what you are buying.

04

After the Registry

Mutation, possession, loan paperwork and resale guidance continue after the money has moved. The relationship is not the transaction.
