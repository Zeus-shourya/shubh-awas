Forteasia, Rohtak

The development

Forteasia is a planned residential development in Rohtak offering both plotted inventory and independent floors. It suits buyers who want the structure of an organised layout — defined roads, planned plot sizes, services laid out in advance — without committing to a full township package and its maintenance.

Who it suits

  • Building your own home on a plot in a planned layout rather than a scattered location.
  • Buying a floor and moving in without a construction project.
  • Holding a plot in an organised development, which generally resells more easily than unplanned land.

What we will tell you

What is actually built on the ground today. Which parts of the layout we think are worth the premium and which are not. How the approvals stand. And if we think another address suits your budget better, we will say that too — including if it means we do not make the sale.

No prices or plot availability are published here. Both move weekly, and a stale figure on a website helps nobody. Message us and you will get the current position for the size you want.

Before you pay anything

Every purchase we handle goes through the same checks — title chain, approvals, encumbrance, and the plot verified on the ground against the layout plan. See residential plots for what that involves in full, and loan and registry support for the paperwork side.

Common questions

What is available in Forteasia right now?

Availability changes week to week, so we do not publish it. Message or call and we will tell you exactly what there is in the size and budget you are after, as of today.

Can I visit the site?

Yes, and you should. We do site visits on the ground at the actual location with the layout plan open - not in an office looking at a render. Tell us when suits you.

Ask about availability
